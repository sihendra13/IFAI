Manifesto & Ecosystem

Where Art and Intelligence Converge.

Indonesia Future Arts & Intelligence (IFAI) is a non-profit foundation and movement creating a sanctuary for local creators to showcase, learn, and sculpt tomorrow's digital heritage. We believe artificial intelligence is not a replacement for human soul, but an infinitely expandable medium.

01

Our Vision

Redefining creativity in Indonesia through the seamless fusion of art and AI. Inspiring creators to see algorithms not as competitors, but as high-velocity collaborator brushes in imagination.

02

Our Mission

Build a thriving, interconnected ecosystem for AI creators across all islands. Showcase groundbreaking works combining art and deep intelligence, offering incubation grants and global stages.

03

Curators & Jury

A diverse council of visionary creative directors, neural technologists, and cultural anthropologists who ensure every piece showcased reflects innovation, technical depth, and artistic integrity.

04

The Story of IFAI

Founded in 2025, IFAI was born from a shared vision among creators who saw AI as a brand-new semantic language of expression. Today, we bridge creators, brands, and public audiences together.
